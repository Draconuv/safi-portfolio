About

I'm a product designer working where the connection is unreliable and the device is old. That constraint is the point: it forces clarity. I build design systems that scale, and flows that survive offline without feeling broken.

Primarily Figma, from tokens and components to full flows and interaction studies. Accessibility, typography, and offline-first patterns are the baseline, not the bonus.

  • Capabilities Product design, design systems, interaction
  • Tools Figma, Rive, handoff
  • Focus Offline-first products, design systems
  • Based Syria, UTC+4
