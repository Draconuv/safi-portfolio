About

I'm a UI/UX designer based in Syria, building products that solve real problems for people with limited connectivity and low-tech devices. I believe design should be invisible — clear interfaces that just work.

I work primarily in Figma, from design systems and component libraries to full screen flows and animation. I pair research with rapid prototyping, and I care deeply about accessibility, RTL typography, and offline-first patterns.
